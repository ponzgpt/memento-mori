# Roadmap / Future Direction (not yet built)

Memento Mori is currently a free, source-available MVP (Apache-2.0). There is no paid product, installer, or payment integration today.

The long-term idea, if the project matures past MVP: keep the source open and free for technical users, and eventually offer a convenience-packaged installer for non-technical users who don't want to build/configure it themselves. Any paid offering would preserve the Apache-2.0 source and keep the paid value strictly limited to installer convenience — not features, not support, not accuracy claims (this project makes no medical, actuarial, or legal claims).

This is a direction, not a commitment or a timeline.
