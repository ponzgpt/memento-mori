Method and limits

Useful because it's honest.

An exact figure would be false precision. That's why we show the method, a wide range, and the limits, right inside the product.

01

Public reference

We use life expectancy at birth from the World Bank WDI indicator SP.DYN.LE00.IN, 2024 values.

02

Transparent calculation

Birth date + country reference + six lifestyle factors = central horizon. We add ±7 years to avoid presenting a certainty that doesn't exist.

03

Local privacy

The calculation happens in your browser. No account, no database, no analytics, and no profile sent to a server.

04

Not professional advice

This is not an individual prediction, and not medical, actuarial, insurance, legal, or mental-health advice.
